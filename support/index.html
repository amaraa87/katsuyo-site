Frequently asked questions

How does the review schedule (SRS) work?

Katsuyō uses FSRS, a spaced-repetition algorithm. Every verb form you learn becomes its own review card (for example, 書く in the て form). After each answer, the app estimates how long you will remember that card and schedules the next review for just before you would forget it.

  • A fast, correct answer pushes the card far into the future.
  • A slow, correct answer brings it back a little sooner.
  • A wrong answer brings it back soon.

Getting one form of a verb right also gives a little credit to the other forms of the same verb. Cards that are due appear in the Review tab, and reviews are played through the same drill games as lessons.

What do the furigana and romaji switches do?

Both are in Settings under Script display, and each can be on or off independently.

  • Furigana shows small kana above kanji, so you can read words like 書かく before you know the kanji.
  • Romaji shows the reading in Latin letters (kaku).

They only change what is displayed. Answers are always chosen in kana, so turning them off is a good way to check your reading as you progress.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app erases it. Delete Katsuyō from your Home Screen, then reinstall it from the App Store to start fresh. If you later restore your iPhone from a backup made before that, the old progress can come back with it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Lessons, all six drill games, reviews, streaks, audio, the “Why?” explanations and your weekly plan all run on your iPhone. The app never connects to the internet.

Which devices are supported?

Katsuyō is an iPhone app and needs iOS 17 or later.

I can't hear any audio.

Katsuyō speaks Japanese with your iPhone's built-in voice. Check that the ringer is not on silent and the volume is up. If the voice sounds wrong or is missing, open iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Japanese and download a voice.

Does it cost anything?

No. Katsuyō is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
